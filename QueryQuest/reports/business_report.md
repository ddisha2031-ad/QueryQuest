## Recommended actions
1. Prioritize the Critical and High-priority overdue backlog through a daily escalation review.
2. Investigate the lowest-SLA categories and teams; validate whether staffing, routing, or ticket complexity drives delays.
3. Review first-response workflows for tickets waiting more than eight hours.
4. Monitor CSAT for the lowest-scoring categories and validate drivers before making process changes.
## Limitations
Data is synthetic. SLA compliance for unresolved tickets uses the configured reporting timestamp; satisfaction is missing for some tickets and is not imputed. Correlations do not establish causation.
