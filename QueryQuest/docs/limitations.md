# Limitations
- All data is synthetic and generated with seed 42; findings demonstrate methodology, not real company performance.
- Satisfaction scores are missing for some tickets and are not imputed.
- SLA compliance for open tickets depends on the reporting timestamp and assumes no interim SLA pauses.
- Agent comparisons do not adjust for ticket complexity, shift coverage, or assignment policy.
- Associations between response/resolution time and CSAT do not prove causation.
